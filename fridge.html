Bite by Bite

Our feeding plan · toddler years
No pressure. No bribes. No backup meal.
We decideWhat is served, when, and where.
Your toddler decidesWhether they eat, and how much.

The six rules

  • 1
    3 meals + 2 snacks, every 2.5–3 hrs. Water only between.
  • 2
    No grazing. Milk with meals, ~16 oz a day max.
  • 3
    Family style. Bowls on the table; they serve themselves.
  • 4
    One safe food on every plate — part of the meal, not a rescue.
  • 5
    No short-order cooking. One meal for everyone.
  • 6
    Say nothing about their eating. Good or bad.

Do

  • Describe — “crunchy and sweet” — don’t sell it.
  • Eat it yourself, visibly, with enjoyment.
  • Accept “no” the first time.
  • Let them spit it out. Escape routes make trying safe.
  • Serve a one-bite portion next to a favorite.
  • End at ~20 minutes, calmly.

Don’t

  • No dessert as payment.
  • No praise for eating. It makes food a performance.
  • No negotiating bites.
  • No backup meal.
  • No hiding veg as the main plan.
  • Don’t let one rough meal change anything.

Bridges from current favorites

Cottage cheese → ricotta → soft egg curds → scrambled egg → egg salad

Pizza → minced pepperoni → meatball → chicken meatball → cut chicken

Pizza sauce → marinara dip → tomato soup → roasted tomato → raw

Peanut butter → on crackers → on apple → as a dip → hummus

Veg: roasted sweet potato → carrot → broccoli → peas → raw

Fruit: applesauce → cooked apple → raw; freeze-dried berry → fresh

Progress — every step counts

on the table→on their plate→touches it→smells it→licks it→spits it out→one bite→eats it

Call the pediatrician if

Fewer than ~20 accepted foods or the list is shrinking · a whole food group or texture refused · gagging, choking, or vomiting at meals · pocketing food · weight loss or falling off the growth curve · panic at the sight of food · signs of reflux, constipation, or pain. Ask for a pediatric feeding therapist (SLP or OT). Early help is short help.

It takes 10–15 tries. Count how often we serve it — not how much gets eaten.
